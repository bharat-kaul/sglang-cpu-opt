VERDICT: at B=32 warm the decode is ~63% OpenMP BARRIER SPIN (load imbalance across 32 threads over 56
uneven-row experts + many small parallel regions), NOT kernel compute (~9%). The MXFP4 unpack-efficiency
lever I was about to pursue targets only ~9% of the step. The real lever is LOAD BALANCING / BARRIER REDUCTION
in the MoE (and decode) parallel regions: balance parallel_2d by actual work (rows) not block count, and/or
reduce the number of parallel-region barriers per step. This also explains the feasibility bench gap
(pure stream 235 = balanced; kernel 100 = imbalanced + barrier tails).
