Why it is reliable — every number is measured, every step is gated

Measured, not assumedCeilings come from uPP on the real silicon, with freq/ISA self-checks — priors are regenerated per node.
Gate at every stageAnalytical → empirical → isolation. No kernel is authored without a user-reviewed feasibility gate.
Certify = eliminateEach operator is certified at its roofline in isolation, removing it as a suspect; the e2e residual is then provably framework / inter-kernel.
Correctness firstA slow-but-correct reference oracle ships first; per-layer parity + task accuracy gate every fast path.
Honest publishingRoofline target vs measured is published with provenance at a labeled config — the gap is stated, not hidden.
